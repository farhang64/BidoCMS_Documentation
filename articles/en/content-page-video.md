# Add video details to a page

Open **Pages**, select the page and language, then choose **Video**. Video rows hold the URLs and duration used by a theme's video section.

## Work in the panel

1. Select **Add Video**.
2. In **embed URL**, enter the provider's embed address when the theme uses an embedded player.
3. In **content URL**, enter the video content address appropriate to the theme.
4. In **Duration (seconds)**, enter the positive whole-number duration. For a two-minute video, use `120`.
5. Select **Save** on the video row.
6. Preview the result on the page to verify the theme displays it and the player works.

Use the row's delete icon and confirm to remove it. Save or delete the correct language's entry. If the panel reports an incorrect duration, enter a value greater than zero. If a video is saved but missing in the preview, check the URLs and whether that page template uses the video section.
