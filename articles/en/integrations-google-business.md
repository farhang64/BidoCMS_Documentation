# Connect Google Business and select locations

Open **Social → Google Business** to manage Google Business Profile locations the connected Google account owns or manages.

## Connect, choose a business and select the branch

1. Select **Connect Google** and complete Google's authorization using the account that manages the Business Profile.
2. After consent, choose **Choose business**. Review the businesses and branches returned by Google.
3. Select one business and choose **Connect this business**. The CMS can then manage that business’s returned locations.
4. When the business has more than one returned location, choose the correct **Branch** before editing, replying to a review or publishing.
5. Switch between available locations with the branch picker. To choose a different business, disconnect the current Google Business connection and complete the connection and selection again.

A connected Google account may return several businesses and locations. CMS submenu grants and provider permissions both affect which features appear. Google may require a verified location for reviews and posts.

## Connection recovery

- If account setup or the app is pending, check with the BidoCMS administrator. Authorizing requires the provider app to be provisioned and the intended Google account to have permission.
- If the business is absent, verify you signed in as its owner or manager, and use **Reload locations** where offered.
- If authorization expired, choose **Reconnect Google**.
- Select **Disconnect** to remove this site's Google Business connection. The Business Profile remains in Google.

The workspace uses Google Business Profile. For its settings and reporting functions use the branch tabs. Maps embedded in a website theme and the page-level Google Search Console and Analytics connections are separate features.
