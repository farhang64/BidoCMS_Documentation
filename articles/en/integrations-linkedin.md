# Connect a LinkedIn profile or company Page

Open **Social → LinkedIn**. The managed workspace opens an account's **Posts** and **Profile** together. Installations connected to a separate LinkedIn service can show additional workspace tabs.

## Connect and select a profile

1. Choose **Connect LinkedIn** and complete LinkedIn authorization.
2. For company Pages, sign in to a member account with appropriate Page roles. Company Page discovery and reading may require LinkedIn Community Management access in the service's app.
3. After authorization, choose the intended personal profile or company Page when several identities are listed.
4. In **Profile**, check the account status, capability tags and Page roles when shown. Use **Sync** to refresh profile details, roles and scopes. New company Page discovery can require another LinkedIn authorization.
5. To add another identity, choose **Add LinkedIn account** where available. To restore an expired or revoked identity, choose **Reconnect LinkedIn**.

**Disconnect** removes the selected local account connection. In the service workspace, **Disconnect authorization** can affect any member and organization accounts derived from that authorization.

## Use Posts and Profile in the managed workspace

- Open **Posts** and choose **Create post** if the selected profile or Page has publishing access. **Publish as** is the selected identity. Enter **Commentary**, up to 3,000 characters, optionally choose **Disable resharing**, and select **Publish now**. The managed composer uses text posts.
- An account with read access shows available LinkedIn posts and **Refresh posts**. **BidoCMS publication history** shows submissions made from the CMS for this account, including for publish-only accounts.
- Review **Details** on a history entry. **Submitted** indicates LinkedIn accepted a post identifier. Final visibility can be confirmed with **Verify on LinkedIn** when read access is available.
- A successful post can offer **Edit post** or **Delete from LinkedIn** according to its permissions. Editing changes the LinkedIn post text. Deleting removes the provider post and retains its local history state.

## If a feature is unavailable

Publishing, reading, media and engagement are separate provider capabilities. Compare the account's visible capabilities and Page role with the attempted action. If **LinkedIn app setup is pending**, check with the administrator responsible for the installation.
