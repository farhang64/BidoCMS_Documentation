# Find and open a Social workspace

Open **Social** to manage the connected profiles and business locations supported by your installation. Work takes place inside the selected provider, and where multiple profiles or branches exist, inside the selected identity.

## Choose a provider

| Provider | Where to work |
| --- | --- |
| Google Business | Business and branch selection, Profile, Hours, Attributes, Reviews, Posts, Photos, Services, Bookings & links, Performance and Google tools. |
| Facebook | Connected Facebook Pages with Posts, Inbox and Profile. |
| Instagram | Connected Instagram accounts with Posts, Reels, Inbox and Profile, plus Stories and Insights where allowed. |
| X (Twitter) | Connected X accounts with Posts, Inbox and Profile. |
| LinkedIn | Connected profiles and Pages. Managed installations show Posts and Profile for the selected account. Other installations may expose a service workspace with Overview, Accounts, Publish, Content, Media and Activity. |
| Telegram | Coming soon in this panel version. |
| WhatsApp | Coming soon in this panel version. |

## Check identity, permission and connection status

1. Click the provider tab.
2. For providers with several connected accounts, confirm the selected account or Page. Google Business uses a selected business and, where applicable, a branch picker.
3. Use the provider's Profile or Overview to check current connection and available capabilities. Connection setup or reconnect may require a different CMS role.
4. Choose the content, inbox, reporting or settings section you need. Before publishing or replying, confirm the account and resulting destination.

A role may grant access to Social without granting every provider or every submenu. A provider may require a configured app, approved scopes or an eligible profile type for specific features. If the workspace shows **app setup is pending**, ask the BidoCMS administrator to check provisioning. If a connected account needs authorization again, use its provider-specific reconnect control. If the same section works for another account, compare the two accounts' provider scopes and roles before changing the CMS role.
